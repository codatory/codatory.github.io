This policy covers Codatory's Scribbles at `www.codatory.page` and its site redirects.

## Visiting the site

You can read the site without an account. I don't use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising scripts on these pages.

[GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) hosts the site and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Cloudflare handles the redirect from `codatory.page` to `www.codatory.page` and may process request information such as your IP address. For details, see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. I use that information to respond and may keep the message as part of my correspondence. You can also email me with privacy questions or to ask me to delete your message.

Contact me at [hello@codatory.com](mailto:hello@codatory.com).

## Links to other sites

Other sites have their own privacy practices. Some older posts link to products using Amazon affiliate links. If you follow one, Amazon may use tracking to identify the referral.
